# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, and shares the spirit of the
[Ubuntu Code of Conduct](https://ubuntu.com/community/ethos/code-of-conduct).

In short: be considerate and respectful, assume good intent, welcome
newcomers and people with every level of experience, and keep discussion
about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Sneha Nagmoti, at
snehanagmoti2004@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues and may ban contributors
who do not follow this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
